A platform successor the client can run confidently for the next fifteen years.

The client has operated on their current ERP platform for nearly two decades — an implementation that has served well but is now approaching end of life. Why this, why now

Extended support for the legacy platform sunsets within a fixed window, but the prudent migration window is now — while the organization still has a stable platform, experienced staff, and negotiating leverage. Rushing a cutover late is how projects fail.
